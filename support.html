Verdict — Support

Verdict analyzes powerlifting squat videos on your device and estimates whether the lift would be judged a good lift.

Getting the best results

  • Film in landscape, from the front, with the whole lifter and bar in frame.
  • Use a tripod or stable surface — avoid handheld shake.
  • Make sure the lifter is well lit and not blocked by rack uprights or spotters.
