Verdict — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Short version: Verdict analyzes your lifting videos entirely on your device. Your videos never leave your phone. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own.

Who we are

Verdict is an iOS app that analyzes powerlifting squat videos and estimates whether a lift would be judged a good lift, using machine-learning models that run entirely on your device. It is developed by an independent developer. Contact: stoughfamily1@gmail.com.

What data the app touches — and where it stays

  • Videos you record or import. With your permission, Verdict uses the camera to record lifts and reads videos you choose from your photo library. All analysis happens on your device using Apple's Core ML framework. Videos and analysis results are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Exports. If you choose to export a video or result, it is saved to your photo library or shared through the iOS share sheet — always at your initiation, always under your control.
  • Analysis history. Your past results are stored locally in the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we do NOT collect

We collect nothing. Verdict has no user accounts, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours that the app talks to. We cannot see your videos, your results, or anything else about your use of the app.

Network access

The app's own code makes no network requests. One system-level exception: the first time the app runs, iOS itself may contact Apple's servers to retrieve a decryption key for the app's bundled machine-learning model (Apple's standard Core ML model-encryption mechanism). This request is made by the operating system, is handled under Apple's privacy policy, and does not include your videos or any personal data.

Feedback and diagnostics

  • Feedback is sent through your own mail app, only when you choose to send it. You decide what to write and whether to include your email address. Lift videos are not attached.
  • Diagnostics. The app keeps a small local technical log about model loading (for troubleshooting). You can share it with us via the share sheet if you choose; it contains technical information only — never video frames or personal data.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. Verdict does not use remote push notifications.

Children

Verdict is not directed at children under 13, and since the app collects no data, no personal information from any user — child or adult — is collected.

Your choices

  • Camera and photo-library access can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.
  • All app data lives on your device — deleting the app removes it.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), this policy will be updated here before that version ships, with a new effective date.
